\section{Coda: the instrument, and what is open}
\label{sec:coda}

Every result above was found in a drawing tool before it was proved, and
measured in the tool after. \Moire\ renders a stack by evaluating its counting
map per pixel --- in closed form for the exact and winding rungs, by a
certified windowed search for the fold rung --- and looking up its ink
potential; its envelope view is the sweep of Theorem~\ref{thm:envelope},
integrated in closed form by Theorem~\ref{thm:exact}; its ratio view is the
weighted merit of Definition~\ref{def:merit}, reduced per pixel; and every
view is a measurement with a gate, checked against golden images through the
real GPU on every change. The engineering --- solvers, the zoo method, the
capture pipeline, the field language --- is the companion paper's, and this
one cites it for all of it. The discipline that made the theory possible is
simpler to state: build the object, try it, learn what it does, then go and
prove it; and never quote a number the repository cannot regenerate.

The loop has paid twice in ways it was not asked to. The duty null and the
temporal selection were not sought; each fell out of a theorem and survived
measurement within the hour. The observer theorem was written to make
``third pattern'' formal and turned out to say why combination tones need an
ear, why hard ink has one moir\'e and blurred ink has an observer's worth, and
why the tool's own gradient estimate had been lying below two pixels per
member. That is the standard a theory should meet: hard to vary, and with
reach.

What is open is arranged by that standard. Iterated conditioning --- the
envelope of an envelope along the chain of subtori a continued fraction
names --- is a filtration by the tower property, and the question is whether
the amplitude weight makes the station-within-station self-similarity of a
locking staircase exact. Band-pass front ends are Theorem~\ref{thm:multiplier}
with a window whose transform has no DC, an observer tuned to one rung of the
ladder, which is what a retina is. The visibility spectrum of
Definition~\ref{def:visibility} turned out to be Perron's sequence of
convergent qualities with a threshold on it (Proposition~\ref{prop:stations});
whether the $pq$ price changes the Lagrange spectrum's structure at the
liminf is open. And the edge of chaos of the resonance web --- rational ratios as
closed orbits, badly approximable directions as deserts, the visible drawing
graded by the weight along the boundary between them --- is a sentence here
and a paper elsewhere.
