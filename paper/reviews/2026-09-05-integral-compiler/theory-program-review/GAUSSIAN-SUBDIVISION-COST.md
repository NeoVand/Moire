# A Gaussian filtering cost for a supplied finite-state pattern

2026-09-06. Independently checked derivations. No implementation, benchmark,
fixed-float certificate, or novelty claim. The source contract and exact
weighted queries are those of [SUBDIVISION-FOOTPRINTS.md](SUBDIVISION-FOOTPRINTS.md).
The principal theorem here is one-dimensional.

For this specified family, Gaussian filtering can use a number of prefix
cells and a polynomial degree growing only polynomially in log(1/epsilon).
Its normalized source-moment error has amplification less than four,
independent of degree. Source state, depth, alphabet, coordinate precision
and arithmetic construction remain costs. This is not a claim about
arbitrary recursive materials or game frame time.

## 1. Source and target

Let F:R->[0,1] be periodic with period one, generated by a supplied
deterministic S-state, base-b digit machine through depth m, b>=2.
The target is I=E F(X), X~N(mu,sigma^2), sigma>0. No Gaussian or ensemble
substitution is made for F. At depth m it is constant on every leaf.

For a prefix state s with ell remaining digits, normalized Bernstein
moments of degree J are

\[
 V_j^{(\ell)}(s)=(J+1)\int_0^1B_j^J(u)f_{s,\ell}(u)\,du,
 \qquad 0\le V_j^{(\ell)}(s)\le1.
\]

The preceding note supplies their exact row-stochastic recurrence.
There are S(J+1) components and at most b(J+1) summands per augmented
row. We use these exact source queries to integrate a polynomial
approximation to the Gaussian kernel.

## 2. Local Gaussian polynomial and its Bernstein coefficients

Consider a cell of width h<=sigma and center c. Put
H=h/sigma, y=(c-mu)/sigma and x=c+h(u-1/2), u in [0,1]. Then

\[
 p(x)=p(c)e^{z(u)},\qquad
 z(u)=-Hy(u-1/2)-\frac{H^2}{2}(u-1/2)^2.
\]

In the degree-two Bernstein basis the coefficients of z are

\[
 \left(Hy/2-H^2/8,\quad H^2/8,\quad -Hy/2-H^2/8\right).
\]

Their maximum absolute value, also attained by |z| at an endpoint, is

\[
 M_C=H|y|/2+H^2/8.
 \tag{1}
\]

For integer n>=0, define P_C=p(c)sum_{a=0}^n z^a/a!, of degree J=2n.
The exponential Taylor remainder gives

\[
 |p-P_C|\le p(c)e^{M_C}\frac{M_C^{n+1}}{(n+1)!}.
 \tag{2}
\]

Bernstein multiplication coefficients are convex combinations of products:
the weights for B_i^a B_j^b at index i+j are
binom(a,i)binom(b,j)/binom(a+b,i+j), summing to one over i+j fixed.
Degree elevation is also a convex combination. Consequently, writing
P_C=sum_{j=0}^J beta_{C,j} B_j^J,

\[
 \max_j|\beta_{C,j}|
 \le p(c)\sum_{a=0}^nM_C^a/a!
 \le p(c)e^{M_C}.
 \tag{3}
\]

This is a maximum-coefficient bound, not a coefficient l1-sum bound.
The Bernstein coefficients can have either sign.

## 3. A global coefficient bound independent of degree and reach

For any disjoint midpoint cells with h_C<=sigma,

\[
 \mathcal A:=\sum_C h_Cp(c_C)e^{M_C}
 \le 2e^{1/4}(1+1/\sqrt{2\pi})<4.
 \tag{4}
\]

To prove this, H<=1 implies

\[
 p(c)e^{M_C}\le e^{1/4}
 [p_{\mu+\sigma/2,\sigma}(c)+p_{\mu-\sigma/2,\sigma}(c)].
\]

For either shifted Gaussian q, the midpoint error on a cell is at most
(h_C/2)integral_C|q'|. Hence

\[
 \sum_C h_Cq(c_C)
 \le\int_{\cup C}q+\frac{\sigma}{2}\int_{\cup C}|q'|
 \le1+1/\sqrt{2\pi}.
\]

This proves (4) without a reach-dependent coefficient factor.

The exact polynomial query on one prefix cell is

\[
 Q_C=\frac{h_C}{J+1}\sum_j\beta_{C,j}V_j^{(m-k)}(s_C).
\]

If every queried V has certified absolute error at most delta, the summed
moment-error contribution is less than 4 delta. If in addition

\[
 \max_j|\widehat\beta_{C,j}-\beta_{C,j}|
 \le\zeta\,p(c_C)e^{M_C},
\]

the combined contribution, before final summation rounding, is at most

\[
 4[\delta+\zeta(1+\delta)].
 \tag{5}
\]

Thus polynomial degree does not force exponentially accurate source
moments in this representation. Parameter formation, coefficient
formation and summation still require their own certificates.

## 4. Reach and degree with explicit constants

Reserve an analytic error allowance 0<epsilon<1; numerical allowances
will be added separately. Set

\[
 R=\sqrt{2\log(4/\epsilon)},\qquad M_*=R/2+3/8.
\]

Retain whole prefix cells intersecting [mu-R sigma,mu+R sigma].
For h<=sigma their centers satisfy |c-mu|<=R sigma+h/2, so M_C<=M_*.
The omitted Gaussian mass is at most
2Phi(-R)<=2exp(-R^2/2)=epsilon/2.

By (2) and (4), the sum of retained-cell L1 kernel errors is at most
4 M_*^N/N!, N=n+1. A sufficient choice is

\[
 N\ge\left\lceil\max\left\{
 1,\ 2eM_*,\ \log_2(8/\epsilon)
 \right\}\right\rceil,\qquad n=N-1,\quad J=2n.
 \tag{6}
\]

Indeed N!>=(N/e)^N and N>=2eM_* give M_*^N/N!<=2^{-N}.
The kernel contribution is then at most epsilon/2. Since F is in [0,1],
the same L1 error bounds its pixel error. Total analytic error is at most
epsilon and J=O(log(1/epsilon)). Holding R fixed while tightening epsilon
would leave a tail floor and would not establish this statement.

## 5. Prefix depth, broad footprints, and exact leaves

For sigma>=1, the wrapped Gaussian satisfies

\[
 {\rm TV}(p_{\rm wrapped},{\rm Haar})
 \le2e^{-2\pi^2\sigma^2}.
\]

This follows from its positive Fourier-tail bound in
[PHASE-QUOTIENT-AND-COMPOSITION.md](PHASE-QUOTIENT-AND-COMPOSITION.md).
If sigma>=sigma_H=max(1,R/(2pi)), the full Haar automaton mean already
has error at most epsilon/2. This is an unconditional phase-only
statement; it is not a conditional law for a resolved prefix.

Otherwise set

\[
 k_*=\max(0,\lceil\log_b(1/\sigma)\rceil),\qquad
 k=\min(m,k_*),\qquad h=b^{-k}.
\]

If k=m, use exact Gaussian masses times constant leaf values. In
particular, when m<k_*, h can exceed sigma and the polynomial bound
must not be applied. For m=0 the source is globally constant.
In the remaining branch k<m, h<=sigma and (1)-(6) apply.

The retained-cell count obeys

\[
 K\le
 \begin{cases}
 2bR+2,&\sigma<1,\\
 R^2/\pi+2,&1\le\sigma<\sigma_H.
 \end{cases}
 \tag{7}
\]

In the first case h>=sigma/b, including the leaf fallback.
In the second h=1 and sigma<R/(2pi). Thus
K<=max(2bR,R^2/pi)+2, or
O(b sqrt(log(1/epsilon))+log(1/epsilon)).
There is no enumeration of all b^m source leaves.

## 6. Arithmetic price and a numerical-error contract

At remaining depth ell=m-k, preparation of all source moments through
degree J takes O(ell b S(J+1)^2) arithmetic, with O(S(J+1)) working
storage when intermediate levels are not retained. Preparing subdivision
tables by positive binomial convolutions costs O(b(J+1)^3) conservatively.

For each retained cell, prefix traversal costs O(k). Its Gaussian
polynomial can be formed in O(n^2) arithmetic and O(n) working storage:
start q_0=S_0=1; form q_a=zq_{a-1}/a in degree 2a, then add it to the
degree-elevated S_{a-1}. Quadratic multiplication and elevation by two
degrees each cost O(a). Contract against V in O(J).

The polynomial branch therefore has the sufficient work bound

\[
 O\left(
 b(J+1)^3+\ell bS(J+1)^2+
 K[k+(J+1)^2]\right),
 \tag{8}
\]

plus the parameter and elementary-function evaluations.
The leaf branch uses O(Kk) transition work and O(K) Gaussian interval
evaluations. The broad branch uses the Haar suffix recurrence.
Homogeneous augmented-operator powering is an alternative with its
own matrix and bit costs; it does not erase the construction cost.

Let B be the exact augmented row-stochastic source operator and let
Bhat be certified nonnegative and row-stochastic with
||Bhat-B||_infinity<=eta_B (maximum absolute row sum).
If each source update has sup-norm rounding error at most tau_V,
then the exact recurrence's values in [0,1] and contractivity give

\[
 \delta_V\le\delta_g+\ell(\eta_B+\tau_V).
 \tag{9}
\]

This is linear, not exponential, accumulation with depth. If Bhat is
not certified stochastic, its norm growth must be included. There
are at most b(J+1) summands per row. A standard bounded-rounding
analysis can aim for tau_V=O(b(J+1)u), but this does not assert that
an existing floating implementation meets the contract.

The exact subdivision weights have denominator b^{J+1}. Table formation,
certified quantization, source payoff precision and update error are
separate. With controlled arithmetic their required precision depends
logarithmically on depth and inverse tolerance; exact rational evaluation
has larger bit costs, and ordinary fixed-float arithmetic is not certified.

For coefficient formation, bound the error in the scaled Gaussian
prefactor and the three quadratic coefficients, then propagate it through
the convex Bernstein products/elevations. For example, stage bounds
tau M_C^a/a! for q_a and tau sum_{j<=a}M_C^j/j! for the addition/elevation
give final coefficient error at most
2n tau p(c)sum_{j<=n}M_C^j/j!, before input errors. This is a sufficient
contract to establish, not a claim that cancellation has disappeared.

Finally add the kernel remainder, discarded tail, (5), and final
contraction/summation error. Coordinate and digit precision, certified cell
selection, exponential evaluation, scaling, and underflow/overflow remain
obligations. A small sigma or large mu can make parameter representation
demanding even when (8) has few cells.

## 7. Shading and higher-dimensional extensions are conditional

For a real polynomial shading factor L and retained union U, exact
integration of L times the polynomial Gaussian model leaves the error

\[
 \|L\|_{\infty,U}\|p-P\|_{L^1(U)}
 +\|L\|_{L^2(p)}\sqrt{P(X\notin U)}.
 \tag{10}
\]

It does not make the original weighted target exact. A degree-p_L
factor raises the needed source-moment degree to J+p_L. On a normalized
reach of radius R, its coefficient bounds and fixed degree bound the
local supremum polynomially in R; the signed tail must receive its own
allocation. If L approximates a different shading response, add that
approximation's error. Projective geometry remainders remain separate
and retain their bounded-observable hypotheses.

For a correlated two-dimensional Gaussian and an actual prefix cell
x=c+Hu, |u_i|<=1 with diagonal half-width matrix H, write

\[
 p(c+Hu)=p(c)e^{v\cdot u-u^TKu/2},\qquad
 v=-H\Sigma^{-1}(c-\mu),\quad K=H\Sigma^{-1}H.
\]

The exponent is bounded by
A_C=|v_1|+|v_2|+(K_{11}+K_{22}+2|K_{12}|)/2.
Exponential truncation after n and exact polynomial shading of degree p_L
need moments through degree 2n+p_L. If all retained cells have A_C<=A_0
and |L|<=Lambda there, the local weighted kernel error is at most
Lambda e^{2A_0} A_0^{n+1}/(n+1)!. This follows from
sum_C |C|p(c)<=e^{A_0}. Signed tails and the actual cell count still matter.

Whitening changes the source's subdivision axes. Fixed-aspect quadtree
refinement, state access, covariance, prefix counts and coefficient
precision must be priced in those actual coordinates. The degree-independent
constant four and the one-dimensional count (7) have not been asserted
for this extension.

The result completes a Gaussian cost/error construction for the supplied
one-dimensional finite-state family. Extending the admitted material
representation, handling general shading and visibility, and establishing
GPU cost are further work.
